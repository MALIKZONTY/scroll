# Mind-blowing — batch 1 (history, science and trivia that's well documented)
Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
Oxford University was already teaching students before the Aztec city of Tenochtitlan was founded.
Woolly mammoths were still alive when the Great Pyramid of Giza was being built.
Honey found in ancient Egyptian tombs was still edible thousands of years later.
Sharks are older than trees. They've been around for over 400 million years.
Bananas are berries, but strawberries aren't.
There are more possible chess games than there are atoms in the observable universe.
The Eiffel Tower can grow about 15 cm taller in summer because the metal expands in the heat.
Hot water can sometimes freeze faster than cold water. It's called the Mpemba effect, and scientists still argue about why.
Your stomach gets a new lining every few days, so it doesn't digest itself.
You are taller in the morning than at night, by about a centimetre.
A group of flamingos is called a flamboyance.
Wombat poop is cube-shaped.
The inventor of the Pringles can was buried with some of his ashes in one.
Scotland's national animal is the unicorn.
There was a war between Britain and Zanzibar in 1896 that lasted less than an hour.
The shortest war in history, the Anglo-Zanzibar War, lasted about 38 minutes.
Nintendo was founded in 1889, as a playing card company.
Before the alarm clock, some people were paid to tap on windows to wake others up. They were called knocker-uppers.
The first oranges weren't orange. Many were green, and some still are in tropical countries.
Carrots weren't always orange either. Purple and yellow carrots came first.
The dot over a lowercase "i" or "j" is called a tittle.
The word "set" has one of the longest entries of any word in the Oxford English Dictionary.
Chess originated in India, from an ancient game called chaturanga.
Snakes and Ladders came from India too, from a game called Moksha Patam.
The number zero as a full number was developed by Indian mathematicians. Brahmagupta wrote rules for it in 628 CE.
Shampoo comes from the Hindi word "chāmpo," meaning to press or massage.
India's Kumbh Mela is so big that the crowds have been photographed from space.
The Indian railways employ over a million people, making them one of the largest employers in the world.
A single cloud can weigh more than a hundred elephants.
Lightning can strike the same place twice. The Empire State Building gets hit around 20 times a year.
Peanuts aren't nuts. They're legumes, like peas and beans.
Tomatoes are fruits, botanically speaking.
Your nose can tell apart a huge number of smells, possibly more than a trillion according to one study.
The human body has about 37 trillion cells.
There are more bacterial cells living in and on you than there are stars in the Milky Way.
The heart beats around 100,000 times a day.
If you stretched out all the blood vessels in your body, they'd circle Earth more than twice.
Your brain generates enough electricity to power a small LED bulb.
Babies are born with about 300 bones. Adults have 206, because some fuse together.
The strongest muscle in the body, by force for its size, is often said to be the jaw's masseter.
Fingernails grow faster than toenails.
Humans are the only animals known to blush.
You can't hum while holding your nose closed. Try it.
Sloths can hold their breath longer than dolphins, up to about 40 minutes.
Koalas have fingerprints so similar to ours they could confuse a crime scene.
A blue whale's heart is roughly the size of a small car.
The Leaning Tower of Pisa took about 200 years to build and started leaning during construction.
The Great Pyramid was the tallest human-made structure on Earth for about 3,800 years.
The Statue of Liberty used to be a shiny copper colour. It turned green from oxidation.
Mount Everest's summit is made of rock that was once at the bottom of an ocean. Marine fossils have been found there.
The first computer bug was a real moth stuck in a Harvard computer in 1947.
The word "robot" comes from a Czech word meaning forced labour.
"OK" started as a joke abbreviation for "oll korrect" in 1830s Boston.
The QWERTY keyboard layout was designed for typewriters in the 1870s, and we're still using it.
The first photo ever taken needed an exposure of several hours, maybe even days.
The first YouTube video, "Me at the zoo," was uploaded in April 2005 and is only 19 seconds long.
The @ symbol was used in trade documents centuries before email existed.
The original name for Google was "BackRub."
An average person spends about a third of their life asleep.
The longest recorded flight of a chicken is 13 seconds.
Water can boil and freeze at the same time, at a special point called the triple point.
Glass is a solid, not a slow liquid. Old windows aren't thicker at the bottom because glass flowed.
Light can be slowed down to walking speed in special lab materials.
There's enough DNA in your body to stretch from the Sun to Pluto and back several times.
If you could fold a sheet of paper 42 times, it would reach the Moon. In reality, you can't fold paper more than about 7 times by hand.
Humans glow faintly in the dark, but the light is about 1,000 times too weak for our eyes to see.
The Moon has moonquakes, and they can last much longer than earthquakes.
Some turtles can breathe through their bums.
The fingerprint pattern on your fingers was set before you were born.
