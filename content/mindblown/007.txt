# Mind-blowing — batch 7: odd facts & things you never noticed
The Eiffel Tower leans slightly away from the sun on hot days as one side expands.
A day on Mars is only about 40 minutes longer than a day on Earth.
The Moon has a small amount of water ice in its permanently shadowed craters.
The International Space Station has been home to people from over 20 countries.
Astronauts can't cry properly in space because tears don't fall.
The Statue of Liberty's full name is "Liberty Enlightening the World."
The Great Wall of China is not a single wall but a series of walls built over centuries.
There's a town in Norway called Hell, and it does freeze over.
Australia is wider than the Moon.
Russia has a larger surface area than Pluto.
The shortest commercial flight in the world, in Scotland, lasts about 90 seconds.
A group of owls is a parliament, a group of crows is a murder, and a group of zebras is a dazzle.
A group of pandas is called an embarrassment.
A group of rhinos is called a crash.
A baby puffin is called a puffling.
A baby echidna or platypus is called a puggle.
A male duck is a drake, and a female duck is a hen.
The hottest chilli peppers can be over 300 times hotter than a jalapeño.
India's Bhut Jolokia, or ghost pepper, was once the world's hottest chilli.
The smell of cut grass is a distress signal from the plant.
Bamboo is one of the fastest-growing plants on Earth.
There are more than 3 trillion trees on Earth, about 400 for every person.
A single tree can hold hundreds of species of insects.
Lightning creates natural fertiliser by turning nitrogen in the air into compounds plants can use.
Snowflakes usually have six sides because of how water molecules bond.
The colour orange was named after the fruit, not the other way around.
Before "orange" became common in English, the colour was often called "yellow-red."
"Pink" was once used for a flower before it became a colour name.
Blue was one of the last colours to get its own word in many ancient languages.
Some people can see more colours than others. They're called tetrachromats.
Men are much more likely to be colour-blind than women.
Left-handed people make up about 10% of the population.
Most people have a dominant eye, just like a dominant hand.
Your nose and ears look bigger as you age, partly because of gravity and changes in cartilage.
Humans are the only animals with chins.
You can't lick your own elbow. Most people just tried.
Your fingers don't have muscles. Tendons from your forearm move them.
Your ring finger and middle finger share tendons, which is why the ring finger is hard to lift alone.
Sneezing with your eyes open is very difficult because of a reflex.
Right-handed people tend to chew food on the right side of their mouths.
You spend roughly a year of your life on the toilet, by one estimate.
The average person walks enough in a lifetime to circle Earth several times.
People are more creative at their "non-optimal" time of day, according to one study.
Most people can't tell the difference between bottled water and tap water in blind tests.
Humans share a common ancestor with every living thing on Earth, including mushrooms.
Your body is home to more microbial cells than you might think, roughly as many as your own cells.
The sky on Mars is butterscotch during the day and blue at sunset.
A rainbow seen from an aeroplane can be a full circle.
Moonbows are rainbows made by moonlight. They're rare and look white to our eyes.
There are more stars in the sky than you can count, but on a dark night you can see only about 2,500 at once.
Space is not completely cold. In direct sunlight, an object near Earth can get very hot.
The word "muscle" comes from the Latin for "little mouse," because flexed muscles looked like mice.
"Salary" may come from the Latin word for salt.
"Disaster" comes from Greek and Latin words meaning "bad star."
"Quarantine" comes from the Italian for "forty days."
"Robot" was first used in a 1920 Czech play.
"Nerd" first appeared in a Dr. Seuss book in 1950.
"Selfie" was the Oxford Dictionaries Word of the Year in 2013.
The longest word in many English dictionaries is pneumonoultramicroscopicsilicovolcanoconiosis, a lung disease.
"Typewriter" is one of the longest words you can type using only the top row of a keyboard.
"Rhythm" is the longest common English word without a standard vowel.
The letter "J" was one of the last letters added to the English alphabet.
The ampersand, "&," was once considered the 27th letter of the English alphabet.
"I am" is one of the shortest complete sentences in English.
Velcro, Post-it notes and microwaves all came from someone noticing something by accident.
Sliced bread was first sold in 1928, so "the best thing since sliced bread" is younger than you might think.
The modern toothbrush with nylon bristles first went on sale in 1938.
