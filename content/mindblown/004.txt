# Mind-blowing — batch 4: numbers, language & everyday science
The word "alphabet" comes from the first two Greek letters, alpha and beta.
"Bookkeeper" has three double letters in a row.
"Strengths" is one of the longest English words with only one vowel.
The sentence "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog" uses every letter of the English alphabet.
The most common letter in English is E.
"Queue" is pronounced the same even if you remove its last four letters.
The word "nice" used to mean "foolish" or "silly."
"Goodbye" comes from "God be with ye."
The hashtag symbol is officially called an octothorpe.
The plastic tip at the end of a shoelace is called an aglet.
The little ridge between your nose and upper lip is called the philtrum.
The smell of rain on dry earth has a name: petrichor.
The fear of long words is called hippopotomonstrosesquippedaliophobia. Someone has a sense of humour.
There are more than 7,000 languages spoken in the world today.
India has 22 officially recognised languages in its Constitution, and hundreds more spoken.
Sanskrit is one of the oldest documented languages in the world.
Tamil is one of the oldest living languages, with a literary history of over 2,000 years.
Hindi is one of the most spoken languages in the world.
The words "jungle," "pyjamas," "shampoo," "bungalow" and "thug" all came into English from Indian languages.
"Avatar" comes from Sanskrit, meaning a descent or incarnation.
"Karma," "guru" and "mantra" are Sanskrit words used worldwide.
The number 0 has no Roman numeral.
A "googol" is 1 followed by 100 zeros. Google's name was inspired by it.
There are more possible ways to shuffle a deck of cards than there are stars in the observable universe.
In a room of just 23 people, there's about a 50% chance two share a birthday. It's called the birthday paradox.
In a room of 70 people, that chance rises to over 99.9%.
111,111,111 × 111,111,111 = 12,345,678,987,654,321.
If you add up all the numbers from 1 to 100, you get 5,050.
Pi has been calculated to trillions of digits, but you only need about 40 to measure the observable universe to the width of an atom.
Every odd number has an "e" in it when you spell it in English.
"Forty" is the only number spelled with its letters in alphabetical order.
A "jiffy" is an actual unit of time in physics and computing, though its length depends on who's using it.
One million seconds is about 11.5 days. One billion seconds is about 31.7 years.
If you started counting to a billion at one number per second, it would take over 30 years.
A single sheet of paper folded 103 times would, in theory, be thicker than the observable universe.
The total weight of all ants on Earth is estimated to be about a fifth of all humans' weight.
Sound travels about 4 times faster in water than in air, and even faster through steel.
A sneeze can travel several metres.
Your phone has more computing power than the computers NASA used for the Apollo Moon landings.
The first computer mouse was made of wood.
The first webcam was set up at Cambridge University to check if a coffee pot was empty.
The first item ever sold on eBay was a broken laser pointer.
The "save" icon is a floppy disk, which many people today have never used.
The QWERTY layout was designed partly to stop typewriter keys from jamming.
Wi-Fi doesn't stand for anything. It was a marketing name.
The first video game is often said to be "Tennis for Two," made in 1958.
Pac-Man's shape was inspired by a pizza with a slice missing, according to its creator.
Mario was originally a carpenter, not a plumber.
Tetris was created by a Soviet engineer in 1984.
Minecraft is one of the best-selling video games of all time.
Barcodes were inspired by Morse code drawn in the sand.
The first product scanned with a barcode was a pack of chewing gum, in 1974.
Velcro was inspired by burrs sticking to a dog's fur.
Matches were invented after lighters. The first lighter came in the 1820s.
The fax machine was invented before the telephone.
The Ottoman Empire and the Aztec Empire existed at the same time.
The University of al-Qarawiyyin in Morocco, founded in 859, is often called the oldest existing university.
Woolly mammoths survived on Wrangel Island until about 4,000 years ago.
Harvard University was founded before calculus was invented.
The Leaning Tower of Pisa has never fallen, and it's actually less tilted now after restoration.
The Eiffel Tower was once painted reddish-brown, and it gets repainted roughly every 7 years.
A bolt of lightning lasts a fraction of a second, but it can heat the air to around 30,000 °C.
Clouds look light, but an average cumulus cloud can weigh around 500 tonnes.
Honey is made from flower nectar that bees regurgitate and dry out.
A strawberry has about 200 seeds on the outside.
Apples float because about a quarter of their volume is air.
Ketchup leaves the bottle faster if you tap the "57" on the neck of a Heinz bottle.
