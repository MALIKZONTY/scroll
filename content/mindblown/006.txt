# Mind-blowing — batch 6: sports, music, film & pop culture
Cricket's first international match was played between the USA and Canada in 1844.
The longest Test match, in 1939 between England and South Africa, lasted 10 days and still ended in a draw.
Sachin Tendulkar is the only player to score 100 international centuries.
India won its first Cricket World Cup in 1983, beating the West Indies at Lord's.
The first Cricket World Cup was held in 1975.
A cricket ball must weigh between about 156 and 163 grams in men's matches.
The fastest recorded delivery in international cricket is about 161 km/h, bowled by Shoaib Akhtar.
Kabaddi players must keep chanting "kabaddi" while raiding, all on one breath.
India has won eight Olympic gold medals in men's field hockey, more than any other country.
Neeraj Chopra won India's first Olympic gold in athletics, in javelin, at Tokyo 2020.
Abhinav Bindra won India's first individual Olympic gold, in shooting, in 2008.
The Olympic rings' colours, plus white, were chosen so every national flag had at least one of them, at the time.
Olympic gold medals are mostly silver, coated with gold.
A marathon is 42.195 km because of the 1908 London Olympics route.
Usain Bolt's 100 m world record is 9.58 seconds, set in 2009.
Football is the world's most popular sport, with billions of fans.
The FIFA World Cup trophy is made of 18-carat gold.
Badminton shuttlecocks can be smashed at over 400 km/h, the fastest of any racket sport.
Tennis scoring may come from a clock face: 15, 30, 45, later shortened to 40.
Chess grandmaster Viswanathan Anand was India's first grandmaster, in 1988.
India has more than 80 chess grandmasters.
Gukesh D became the youngest undisputed world chess champion in 2024, at 18.
The Rubik's Cube has over 43 quintillion possible combinations.
Every Rubik's Cube position can be solved in 20 moves or fewer.
The world record for solving a Rubik's Cube is under 4 seconds.
Bollywood produces more films a year than Hollywood, in some years.
Indian cinema, across all languages, is the largest film industry in the world by number of films.
Raja Harishchandra, released in 1913, is considered India's first full-length feature film.
Alam Ara, released in 1931, was India's first sound film.
Satyajit Ray received an honorary Oscar in 1992.
"Naatu Naatu" from RRR won the Oscar for Best Original Song in 2023.
A.R. Rahman won two Oscars for Slumdog Millionaire.
The Telugu film industry is nicknamed Tollywood; the Tamil industry, Kollywood.
Lata Mangeshkar recorded songs in dozens of languages over more than seven decades.
Kishore Kumar was also an actor, director and producer, not just a singer.
The sitar's modern form developed in the 18th century in India.
The tabla is a pair of drums: the smaller dayan and the larger bayan.
Indian classical music has two main traditions: Hindustani and Carnatic.
George Harrison of the Beatles learned the sitar from Ravi Shankar.
The Beatles spent time at an ashram in Rishikesh in 1968.
"Happy Birthday to You" was under copyright until 2016.
The world's best-selling album is Michael Jackson's Thriller.
The first music video played on MTV was "Video Killed the Radio Star."
Mozart started composing music at the age of five.
Beethoven composed some of his greatest works after becoming deaf.
The piano has 88 keys.
The violin has four strings.
A grand piano can have over 10,000 parts.
The first Harry Potter book was rejected by several publishers before it was published.
The Lord of the Rings was originally published in three volumes because of paper shortages after World War II.
Sherlock Holmes never said "Elementary, my dear Watson" in the original books.
Mickey Mouse first appeared in "Steamboat Willie" in 1928.
The first Disney animated feature film was Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, in 1937.
The Simpsons is the longest-running American animated series.
The first Star Wars film was released in 1977.
Avatar is the highest-grossing film of all time, not adjusted for inflation.
Titanic was the first film to earn more than a billion dollars.
Jurassic Park's dinosaur roars were made by mixing animal sounds, including baby elephants and tigers.
The sound of the Velociraptors talking in Jurassic Park came partly from tortoises.
The famous "Wilhelm scream" sound effect has been used in hundreds of films.
Chhota Bheem, the cartoon, became one of India's most popular kids' shows.
Doordarshan, India's public broadcaster, began in 1959.
The TV serials Ramayan and Mahabharat in the late 1980s emptied streets across India on Sunday mornings.
India's first colour TV broadcasts began in 1982, for the Asian Games.
Pokémon is the highest-grossing media franchise in the world.
Minecraft has sold over 300 million copies.
Super Mario Bros. was one of the best-selling games of the 1980s.
Pac-Man has a perfect score of 3,333,360 points.
The first emoji set was created in Japan in 1999.
The "face with tears of joy" emoji was the Oxford Dictionaries Word of the Year in 2015.
The most-viewed YouTube video is "Baby Shark Dance."
"Gangnam Style" was the first YouTube video to reach 1 billion views.
T-Series, the Indian music label, is one of the most-subscribed channels on YouTube.
