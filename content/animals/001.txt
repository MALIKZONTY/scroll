# Animals — batch 1 (well-established facts)
Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood.
Sea otters sometimes hold hands while they sleep so they don't drift apart. 🦦
Cows have strong social bonds and can get stressed when separated from their companions.
Elephants are among the few animals that can recognise themselves in a mirror.
Dolphins call each other by unique whistles that work a lot like names.
A group of crows is called a murder.
Crows can recognise individual human faces and remember people who treated them badly.
Honeybees communicate the location of flowers with a "waggle dance."
Flamingos are pink because of the pigments in the shrimp and algae they eat.
A cheetah can reach about 100 km/h in just a few seconds.
The peregrine falcon is the fastest animal on Earth, diving at over 300 km/h.
Hummingbirds are the only birds that can fly backwards.
A hummingbird's heart can beat over 1,000 times a minute.
Sloths move so slowly that algae grows on their fur.
A sloth may come down from its tree only about once a week, to go to the toilet.
Giraffes have the same number of neck bones as humans: seven.
A giraffe's tongue can be about 50 cm long and is dark purple.
Penguins propose to their mates with a pebble. Some species, like Gentoo penguins, really do this.
Emperor penguin dads keep their eggs warm on their feet for about two months without eating.
Koalas sleep up to 18 to 22 hours a day.
Wombats produce cube-shaped poop.
Axolotls can regrow lost limbs, and even parts of their heart and brain.
Starfish can regrow lost arms.
Starfish don't have brains.
Jellyfish have existed for more than 500 million years, long before dinosaurs.
Some jellyfish, like Turritopsis dohrnii, can revert to a younger stage, earning the nickname "immortal jellyfish."
A blue whale is the largest animal that has ever lived, bigger than any dinosaur.
A blue whale's tongue can weigh as much as an elephant.
Blue whale calls can travel hundreds of kilometres through the ocean.
Tardigrades, tiny "water bears," can survive extreme cold, heat, radiation and even the vacuum of space.
Owls can't move their eyes. Instead, they can turn their heads about 270 degrees.
A snail can sleep for up to three years when conditions are too dry.
Cats spend around two-thirds of their lives sleeping.
A cat's purr may help it heal, according to some researchers.
Cats can't taste sweetness.
Dogs' noses have unique prints, just like human fingerprints.
A dog's sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than ours.
Dogs can understand some human words and gestures, and some dogs learn hundreds of words.
Rats laugh when tickled, but the sound is too high-pitched for us to hear.
Pigs are very intelligent, and can learn to play simple video games with a joystick.
Goats have rectangular pupils that help them see a wide panorama.
Horses can sleep standing up.
Kangaroos can't walk backwards easily.
A baby kangaroo, called a joey, is about the size of a jellybean when it's born.
Male seahorses carry and give birth to the babies.
A shrimp's heart is located in its head.
Butterflies taste with their feet.
Snakes smell with their tongues.
Frogs drink water through their skin.
Some frogs can freeze solid in winter and thaw back to life in spring.
Camels store fat, not water, in their humps.
Polar bears have black skin under their white-looking fur.
Polar bear fur isn't actually white. Each hair is transparent and hollow.
Zebras' stripes are unique to each animal, like fingerprints.
Scientists think zebra stripes may help keep biting flies away.
Elephants can "hear" through their feet, picking up rumbles through the ground.
Elephants mourn their dead and have been seen returning to the bones of relatives.
An elephant's trunk has tens of thousands of muscles.
Bats are the only mammals that can truly fly.
Some bats find their food in total darkness using echolocation.
Vampire bats share blood meals with hungry roost-mates.
A group of owls is called a parliament.
A group of jellyfish is called a smack.
A group of porcupines is called a prickle.
Parrots can live for decades. Some large parrots live over 60 years.
African grey parrots can learn hundreds of words and use some of them meaningfully.
Ants can carry many times their own body weight.
There are roughly 20 quadrillion ants on Earth, according to a 2022 estimate.
Termites build mounds with natural air conditioning.
Bees can recognise human faces in experiments.
A single honeybee makes only about a twelfth of a teaspoon of honey in its lifetime.
The Bengal tiger is India's national animal.
India's national bird is the peacock, and only the males have the famous colourful tail.
The Asiatic lion now lives in the wild only in Gujarat's Gir Forest region.
Tigers have striped skin, not just striped fur.
No two tigers have the same stripe pattern.
Lions sleep up to 20 hours a day.
Dolphins sleep with one half of their brain at a time.
Sea turtles return to the same beach where they hatched to lay their own eggs.
Olive ridley sea turtles nest in huge groups on Odisha's beaches, an event called an arribada.
Hippos can't really swim. They walk or bounce along the bottom of rivers.
Hippos secrete a reddish substance that acts like natural sunscreen.
