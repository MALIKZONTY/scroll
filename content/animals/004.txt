# Animals — batch 4: mammals
Cheetahs can't roar. They purr, chirp and meow like big house cats.
Lions are the only cats that live in large social groups, called prides.
A lion's roar can be heard up to about 8 km away.
Female lions do most of the hunting for the pride.
Snow leopards can't roar either, and they use their long tails as scarves.
Snow leopards live in the high mountains of Central and South Asia, including Ladakh.
Leopards often drag their prey up trees to keep it away from other predators.
Jaguars have one of the strongest bites of any big cat, strong enough to crack turtle shells.
Tigers are the largest cats in the world.
Tigers are good swimmers and often cool off in water.
India is home to most of the world's wild tigers.
Elephants can't jump.
Elephants use their trunks to breathe, smell, drink, grab things and greet each other.
Baby elephants sometimes suck their trunks for comfort, like human babies suck their thumbs.
Elephants can communicate with low rumbles that travel long distances.
An elephant's pregnancy lasts about 22 months, the longest of any land mammal.
Giraffes only need a few hours of sleep a day.
Giraffes give birth standing up. The baby drops about 1.5 metres to the ground.
A giraffe's heart is about 60 cm long and very powerful, to pump blood up its long neck.
Rhinos' horns are made of keratin, the same stuff as your fingernails.
Hippos are among the most dangerous large animals in Africa.
Gorillas are mostly gentle plant-eaters.
Gorillas share about 98% of their DNA with humans.
Chimpanzees use sticks to fish termites out of mounds.
Orangutans spend most of their lives in trees.
Orangutans have been seen using leaves as umbrellas.
Bonobos often resolve conflicts through social bonding rather than fighting.
Monkeys at Jaipur's Galta Ji temple are famous for living among visitors.
Some monkeys in Japan, called snow monkeys, relax in hot springs in winter.
Bears can smell food from kilometres away.
Polar bears can swim for days at a time.
Sloth bears, found in India, love eating termites and can suck them up through a gap in their teeth.
Red pandas aren't closely related to giant pandas.
Red pandas live in the eastern Himalayas, including Sikkim and Arunachal Pradesh.
Giant pandas spend up to about 14 hours a day eating bamboo.
A panda's "thumb" is actually a special wrist bone.
Koalas aren't bears. They're marsupials.
Kangaroos can jump about 3 metres high.
Kangaroos use their strong tails like a fifth leg.
Platypuses are mammals that lay eggs.
Male platypuses have venomous spurs on their back legs.
Echidnas are the only other egg-laying mammals besides platypuses.
Bats can live for decades, an unusually long time for such small mammals.
Fruit bats help plant forests by spreading seeds.
Flying squirrels don't fly. They glide using flaps of skin.
Squirrels forget where they bury many of their nuts, which helps new trees grow.
Squirrels can rotate their back feet so they can climb down trees head-first.
Rabbits' teeth never stop growing.
Rabbits can turn their ears to hear in different directions.
Hamsters stuff food into their cheek pouches to carry it home.
Guinea pigs "popcorn," jumping into the air when they're happy.
Mice can squeeze through gaps about the width of a pencil.
Rats can tread water for hours.
Capybaras are the largest rodents in the world.
Capybaras are so relaxed that other animals often sit on them.
Beavers build dams that can be visible from space.
Beavers' teeth are orange because they contain iron.
Meerkats take turns standing guard while the others forage.
Mongooses can fight cobras, thanks to speed and resistance to some snake venom.
Hyenas' "laugh" is actually a sign of nervousness or excitement.
Wolves howl to communicate with their pack over long distances.
Dogs evolved from ancient wolves.
Dogs can sense some human emotions from our faces and voices.
Some dogs can be trained to sniff out diseases, including certain cancers.
A dog's sense of time may partly come from how smells fade during the day.
Cats have a third eyelid.
Cats have about 30 muscles in each ear.
Cats usually meow to communicate with humans, not with other cats.
A cat's nose print is unique.
Horses can't vomit.
Horses have the largest eyes of any land mammal.
Zebras can't be tamed like horses.
Camels can drink more than 100 litres of water in about 10 minutes.
Camels have three eyelids and two rows of eyelashes to protect against sand.
Cows have four stomach compartments.
Cows can smell things from several kilometres away.
Pigs roll in mud to cool down because they can't sweat much.
Sheep can recognise the faces of other sheep and humans.
Goats have accents. Their bleats can change depending on their social group.
Deer antlers can grow up to about 2.5 cm a day, among the fastest-growing tissue in the animal world.
The blackbuck is one of the fastest antelopes and is found in India.
