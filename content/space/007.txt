# Space — batch 7: top-up
A spoonful of the Sun's core would weigh far more than the same spoonful of water, because the core is extremely dense.
It takes light about 5.5 hours to reach Pluto from the Sun.
Mercury has the most eccentric orbit of the eight planets.
Venus has a thick atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide.
Mars has the largest known volcano and the deepest known canyon in the solar system.
Jupiter's moon Ganymede is the largest moon in the solar system.
Saturn's rings are named in order of discovery, using letters.
Uranus has 13 known rings.
Neptune's moon Triton is one of the coldest known objects in the solar system, at about −235 °C.
Comets lose material every time they pass near the Sun.
Halley's Comet was last seen in 1986.
There are hundreds of thousands of known asteroids.
The first asteroid ever discovered, Ceres, was found in 1801.
Meteorites are often named after the place where they fall.
The Moon's highest mountains are around 5 km tall, measured from their base.
The James Webb Space Telescope launched on Christmas Day 2021.
The Hubble Space Telescope is named after astronomer Edwin Hubble.
